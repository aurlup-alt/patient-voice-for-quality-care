# Patient Voice Case Study Template

## 1. Purpose

This template helps healthcare organisations document how patient feedback has been used to identify problems, implement changes, and improve the quality of care.

Case studies can support organisational learning, knowledge sharing, and the spread of effective quality improvement practices.

A good case study describes not only what the organisation did, but also what patients experienced, what the team learned, and what evidence supports the reported results.

This template can be adapted for hospitals, clinics, primary healthcare facilities, outpatient services, and other healthcare settings.

## 18. Ethics, Privacy, and Responsible Reporting

Before publishing a case study:

- Remove personally identifiable patient information.
- Obtain appropriate permission for identifiable quotations, photographs, or stories.
- Follow applicable consent, confidentiality, data protection, and organisational policies.
- Obtain ethics review or approval where required for the activity and intended publication.
- Avoid making unsupported claims about outcomes or causality.
- Report unfavourable findings and limitations honestly.
- Ensure that patients and communities are represented respectfully.
- Do not publish information that could expose patients to stigma, discrimination, or harm.

Quality improvement documentation and research publication may have different governance requirements. Seek appropriate local guidance when uncertain.

## 20. Key Principle

Patient voice becomes a driver of healthcare quality when organisations move beyond collecting feedback and demonstrate how patients' experiences influence decisions, changes, and learning.

**Listen to patients. Act on what matters. Measure what changes. Share what is learned.**
