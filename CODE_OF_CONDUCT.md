# Code of Conduct

## 1. Our Commitment

Patient Voice for Quality Care is committed to creating a respectful, inclusive, and constructive environment for everyone contributing to the project.

We welcome healthcare professionals, researchers, quality improvement practitioners, patients, caregivers, patient representatives, and other interested contributors, regardless of their professional background, experience, nationality, language, disability, or level of technical expertise.

Our shared purpose is to help healthcare organisations listen to patients and use their experiences to improve healthcare quality, safety, equity, and patient-centred care.

## 2. Expected Behaviour

All participants are expected to:

- Communicate respectfully and professionally.
- Welcome different perspectives and experiences.
- Listen to patients and patient representatives without dismissing their concerns.
- Critique ideas, methods, and evidence constructively rather than attacking individuals.
- Use inclusive and accessible language.
- Respect confidentiality and privacy.
- Acknowledge the contributions of others.
- Provide constructive feedback and remain open to learning.
- Recognise uncertainty and distinguish evidence from personal opinion.
- Support collaboration across professional, cultural, and geographic boundaries.

## 3. Unacceptable Behaviour

The following behaviours are not acceptable:

- Harassment, intimidation, threats, or personal attacks.
- Discrimination based on personal characteristics or background.
- Dismissive or demeaning treatment of patients, caregivers, or contributors.
- Deliberate disclosure of confidential or personally identifiable information without appropriate authorisation.
- Sharing patient records or sensitive healthcare information without a lawful and appropriate basis.
- Publishing misleading claims or knowingly misrepresenting evidence.
- Plagiarism or misrepresenting another person's work as one's own.
- Unwelcome conduct that disrupts constructive participation.
- Retaliation against people who raise concerns in good faith.

Professional disagreement and evidence-based criticism are welcome when expressed respectfully.

## 4. Patient Dignity and Safety

Patient experiences must be treated with dignity and care.

Contributors should not minimise, ridicule, or dismiss patient concerns because of a patient's background, circumstances, or communication style.

When discussing patient stories or healthcare experiences:

- Protect patient identity and confidentiality.
- Obtain appropriate consent and permissions where required.
- Avoid sharing information that could expose a patient to harm or stigma.
- Discuss sensitive experiences respectfully.
- Follow applicable ethical, legal, and organisational requirements.

This repository is intended to support learning and improvement. It is not an appropriate place to publish identifiable patient complaints or confidential clinical records.

## 5. Evidence, Integrity, and Accountability

Contributors should represent information accurately and transparently.

When sharing tools, research, or improvement examples:

- Cite sources where appropriate.
- Respect copyright and licensing conditions.
- Identify important limitations.
- Avoid unsupported claims about effectiveness.
- Distinguish local experience from validated evidence.
- Correct significant errors when they are identified.

Contributions should support learning rather than blame, humiliation, or retaliation.

## 6. Inclusion and Accessibility

We recognise that patient voice and healthcare quality are influenced by differences in language, culture, disability, access to services, and social circumstances.

We encourage contributions that help make patient participation more accessible and representative.

No contributor should be treated as less valuable because of their location, language, professional seniority, or technical experience.

## 7. Raising a Concern

If you experience or observe behaviour that conflicts with this Code of Conduct, raise the concern through the repository's available contact channel or contact the repository maintainer through an appropriate public professional channel.

When raising a concern, provide relevant details without unnecessarily sharing confidential or personally identifiable information.

Concerns will be considered with care and handled as discreetly as reasonably possible. Absolute confidentiality cannot be guaranteed in every circumstance.

## 8. Response to Concerns

The repository maintainer will assess reported concerns and determine an appropriate response.

Depending on the circumstances, possible actions may include:

- Providing guidance or requesting that behaviour change.
- Removing inappropriate content.
- Issuing a warning.
- Temporarily restricting participation.
- Removing a contributor's participation privileges in serious or repeated cases.

Responses should be proportionate to the circumstances and should consider the safety and dignity of those affected.

This project does not replace formal organisational, regulatory, safeguarding, or legal reporting procedures.

## 9. Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to participation in the Patient Voice for Quality Care project, including repository discussions, issues, pull requests, and other project-related spaces managed by the project.

Participants should also act respectfully when representing the project in related public discussions or events.

## 10. Responsibilities of Maintainers

Project maintainers are responsible for explaining these expectations, reviewing concerns brought to their attention, and taking reasonable steps to support a respectful collaborative environment.

Maintainers should apply these principles fairly and consistently while protecting patient privacy and the integrity of the project.

## 11. Acknowledgement

By participating in this project, contributors are expected to follow this Code of Conduct and help maintain an environment in which patients, professionals, researchers, and other contributors can learn from one another.

## 12. Our Shared Principle

Patient voice is essential to understanding whether healthcare is respectful, responsive, safe, and centred on people's needs.

Our collaboration should reflect the same values that we seek to promote in healthcare.

**Respect every voice. Protect every person's dignity. Learn together to improve care.**
